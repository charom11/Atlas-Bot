🚀 Top 5 Areas for Improvement
1. Faster Trend Reversal Detection (1H + 15m Market Structure Shift)
Current State: The macro trend filter relies on the 4-Hour (4H) timeframe, which is very reliable but can lag by a few hours during rapid market reversals.
Upgrade: Add a Dual 1H/15m Market Structure Shift (MSS) trigger. If price breaks a key 1H swing low with high volume, the bot flips its directional bias immediately without waiting for the 4H candle to close.
Benefit: Eliminates the "transition lag" and catches short entries earlier when a bull run tops out.
2. Choppy Market / ADX Regime Filter (Anti-Whipsaw Protection)
Current State: The 31 models evaluate signals continuously, even when volatility is dead flat.
Upgrade: Integrate an ADX (Average Directional Index) & ATR Volatility Gate:
If ADX < 20 (sideways chop / low trend strength), raise the consensus requirement to 31/31 or pause new entries.
If ADX > 25 (strong trend), use normal high-confluence execution.
Benefit: Drastically reduces false breakouts and stop-outs during boring sideways weekend chop.
3. Directional Exposure Cap (Correlation Protection)
Current State: The bot allows up to 5 concurrent positions, which could all be Longs at the same time.
Upgrade: Implement a Directional Portfolio Cap:
Max 3 Longs allowed at once unless previous Longs have already had their Stop-Loss moved to Breakeven.
Benefit: Prevents a sudden Bitcoin flash-crash from stopping out 5 altcoin Longs in the exact same candle.
4. Multi-Stage Scale-Out (3-Stage Profit Locker)
Current State: 50% TP1 scale-out ➔ 50% dynamic trailing runner.
Upgrade: Implement a 3-Stage Scale-Out Engine:
TP1 (33%): Quick 1.5R gain ➔ Move SL to Breakeven (+0.05% fees locked).
TP2 (33%): Structural resistance/support target ➔ Lock in major profit.
TP3 (34% Runner): Dynamic ATR Super-Trend trailing stop to ride macro swings.
Benefit: Locks in profit faster while still keeping skin in the game for super-trends.
5. Automated Daily PnL Telegram Summary Report
Current State: You check /status or /circuit manually on Telegram.
Upgrade: Automatically broadcast a clean daily performance report at 00:00 UTC showing:
Daily starting balance vs ending balance
Total trades taken, Win Rate %, Total Realized PnL ($)
Largest win vs largest loss
